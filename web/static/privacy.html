Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026

Keep Swinging is a session organizer for tennis and padel. This page explains what information is handled when you use the app and the choices you have.

Information you provide

When you start a session you enter player names, and as you play you record match scores. These are stored on the app's server so that the session link can be shared between devices. Sessions are keyed by a random identifier and are automatically deleted after the storage retention period (currently 30 days by default).

The app does not require accounts, and it does not collect email addresses, passwords, or payment information.

Cookies and similar technologies

The core session features do not rely on cookies. Third-party advertising described below may set cookies or use similar technologies in your browser.

Advertising

This site uses Google AdSense to display ads. Google and its partners may use cookies, including the DoubleClick/Google advertising cookie, to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info.

For visitors in the EEA, the UK, or California, a consent message is presented where required so you can choose how your data is used for advertising.

How information is shared

Session data is stored to operate the app and is not sold. Ad serving is handled by Google AdSense as described above.

Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
