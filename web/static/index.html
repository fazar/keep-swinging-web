    Session setup

    Standing “Adj” uses raw + k × (leader GP − your GP). Increase k so players behind on games catch up faster in rankings; set k to 0 to turn off parity. New sessions default to 2 (tennis) or 10 (padel); sessions without a saved multiplier use that same sport default.

    Use Remove to mark someone temporarily away—they stay in history and standings but drop out of matchups until you register the same name again (restore). At least four people must remain in shuffle for doubles.
